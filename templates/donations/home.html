How FoodRescue Works

A streamlined, 4-step redistribution pipeline ensuring food safety, speed, and accountability.

1
Donor Posts Surplus

Restaurants, hostels, or individuals list fresh leftover food, specifying portion counts, preparation timestamp, and expiry window.

2
NGO Requests Food

Verified orphanages and community shelters browse active donations in their locality and place an immediate request.

3
Volunteer Dispatched

A registered volunteer courier accepts the transport mission, picks up hot/fresh meals from the donor, and begins delivery.

4
Delivered to Hungry

Meals arrive safely at the shelter, satisfying hunger, minimizing carbon emissions, and eliminating food wastage.

Why It Matters

The Multiplier Effect of Food Redistribution

Food waste is responsible for nearly 10% of global greenhouse emissions. Simultaneously, millions face acute nutritional insecurity. FoodRescue delivers a win-win for our community and planet:

Zero Landfill Methane Emissions

Diverting edible food from dumps reduces toxic methane release and water wastage.

Dignified Nutrition for Shelters

High-quality, freshly cooked meals nourish children, seniors, and disadvantaged families.

Corporate ESG & Tax Compliance

Hotels and caterers receive digital proof of donation for CSR and food-safety certification.
