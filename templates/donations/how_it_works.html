How FoodRescue Works

Learn how Donors, NGOs, and Volunteers collaborate seamlessly to eradicate food wastage.

Food Donor Workflow

Phase 1
1. List Surplus

Fill in the donation form specifying meal name, category, portion count, safe expiry time, and pickup address.

Phase 2
2. Review Requests

Receive instant notifications when nearby registered NGOs request your food. Accept or decline with a single click.

Phase 3
3. Hand Over to Courier

An assigned volunteer arrives at your door with identification to pick up and transport the package.

NGO & Shelter Workflow

Phase 1
1. Browse Available Food

Filter active listings by city, food category (Cooked, Groceries, Bakery), and check remaining expiry times.

Phase 2
2. Place Request

Specify the portions required and add notes regarding distribution to children or senior citizens.

Phase 3
3. Receive & Distribute

Track courier progress from donor kitchen to your shelter doorstep in real-time.

Join as NGO Receiver →

Volunteer Logistics Workflow

Phase 1
1. Claim Open Pickups

View approved requests waiting for dispatch in your area. Accept the mission that fits your schedule.

Phase 2
2. Collect from Donor

Travel to donor address, inspect meal packing, and tap 'Mark as Collected' to start the transit timer.

Phase 3
3. Deliver & Complete

Deliver food to the receiver NGO, record handover remarks, and complete the redistribution loop.
